Build your own biomes and chain them into whole expeditions - from the plane crash to the summit, played like vanilla PEAK, alone or with friends. Mix in the game's own biomes, roll random expeditions and share them with a code. Plus a material picker for every surface, your own rock blocks, better cave tools and painted scatter areas.

BIOMES
- New content type: biomes. "Create map" › Biome lets you pick a size from S to XXL (with a side view next to the Mesa and a rough play time). You build inside a fixed box between a start and a goal platform - campfire and statue sit at the goal - and can grow the box later. The editor check tells you what sticks out of the box, whether there is a route from start to goal and whether there are too many networked objects. "Finish" records the scattering and draws the height profile and side view.
- Publish it as a biome for expeditions, with its stats (size, length, height gain, chests, height profile). The workshop has a new "Biomes" category and a "BIOME S-XXL" badge.

EXPEDITIONS
- Chain biomes into an expedition and play it like vanilla: each stage has its campfire, statue and fog walls, the fog speed follows the estimated play time, and at the end there is the flare, the helicopter and "YOU PEAKED". Up to 32 biomes, 30 km long and 15 km high. A biome's sky and music cover its stage, climbing music and ambience work like in the game.
- Vanilla biomes in expeditions: shore, tropics, roots, alpine, mesa, caldera (with the Kiln) and gloom (with the citadel) come from the game's real daily maps - pick a variant or let it be random. Light, cold, heat, fog, enemies and items behave like in the game. Loading happens behind the loading screen (3-5 biomes ahead, depending on your RAM) and in the vanilla transition at the campfire, without freezes.
- Expedition planner (Workshop › Maps › "Plan expedition"): add biomes and drag them into order, see the height profile and the rules (too long, too high, duplicate biomes), search, filter and sort the biome list (A-Z, newest, rating). Roll an expedition online, vanilla-only or mixed - every roll gets a code your friends can load. "Hide biomes" keeps the rolled biomes secret, even from the host, until you reach them. Save, delete and publish expeditions, and continue one from the last camp you reached. Fog is always on in expeditions.
- Camps, crash site and summit: every camp uses the same hand-built platform (its rock adapts to the biome next to it, with food skewers at the fire). Expeditions start at the original plane crash with some gear and end on a proper summit.
- Expeditions in multiplayer: the host picks an expedition for the lobby (switch Map | Expedition, also in "Create lobby"). Guests download only the biomes they are missing and assemble the expedition themselves, and the round starts together once everyone has preloaded. Public expedition lobbies show up in the lobby browser, private ones work with a join code. Late joiners get the same expedition and follow the stage changes. "My lobby" hides rules, teams and show for expeditions.

EDITOR
- Material picker for every surface. Rocks, terrain, rock masses and primitives pick their material from one catalog with preview tiles, search and groups (rock, desert, snow, volcano, forest, temple ...), applied with a click. Create your own materials: a tinted variant of a game material (top layer, structure image, structure size, footstep sound) or your own image - other players get the images with the map.
- Your own rock blocks. "Save as own rock" turns a sculpted rock mass into a block you can place, rotate, scale and scatter like any other rock (Blocks › Own rocks). The map carries it. Rock masses also grow their box by themselves while you sculpt, drill or carve rooms.
- Cave tools: a live preview cuts tunnels and rooms into the rock while you aim, and a click keeps them. The sign tells you when the shape lies completely inside the rock (invisible from outside). New cross-sections for tunnels and the brush: round, square, diamond, triangle, arch and pointed arch.
- Paint scatter areas straight onto terrain, the island or scattered objects - things only grow where you paint (Shift erases, Shift+wheel or [ ] changes the brush). The first stroke gives the area its shape. "New area on exactly this piece" and "one layer up with the same outline" put mushrooms and bushes on top of your scattered rocks.
- New block in every map: the original crashed plane (Blocks › Island blocks).

CHANGES
- Simplified Chinese translation updated - it now covers almost the whole mod, including biomes, expeditions and the planner (thanks to TFJSN / ETWML).
- Trees, plants and mushrooms are now scattered upright instead of leaning with the slope.
- Statues bring back late-join ghosts (not in team rounds), like in vanilla.

FIXES
- Number fields in the editor work again. Since the PEAK 3 update you could click into them, but typing and Backspace did nothing - and Delete could remove the selected object instead of a digit.
- Players who join while the host is still loading are now waited for and play normally instead of becoming ghosts.
- Joining a running round by room code no longer sometimes builds the island without the host's map.
- A ghost in the round made every statue a dud - no items, no revive. Fixed.
- Fixed an antlion error spamming the console every frame.
- Players can now load the host's private (link-only) biomes in an expedition. If a biome still can't be loaded, the message names it and says why.
- The lobby list no longer retries a refused map every 15 seconds (which ended in "too many requests").
- Stretched sphere and capsule primitives now collide with their visible shape - no more invisible bubble above flat domes.
