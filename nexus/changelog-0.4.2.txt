Maps now show how often they were played, palms can wear the bark of the beach palms, and joining or rejoining a running round no longer gets stuck.

WORKSHOP
- Play counts. Every map card shows how often the map was played (solo or in a lobby, once per player every 30 minutes; your own maps don't count). The map page shows it under the rating, and "Most played" is a new sort order. Plays are counted from 0.4.2 on.

EDITOR
- Beach palms. Palms can now use the light brown bark of the beach palms, or the dark beach and desert bark: Object › Settings › Bark, or "Palm bark" on a scatter area. Without a choice the palm keeps its grey jungle bark, so existing maps look the same.

FIXES
- Fixed late joiners getting stuck without a character when the round was in a stage without a campfire.
- Fixed rejoining players getting stuck in the loading screen when the round was in a stage without a scout statue.
- Joining a lobby on a map without scatter areas no longer freezes for 20 seconds.
