Walk around planets like in Super Mario Galaxy, build items that launch you, fly, float or shoot ropes, put jump pads and launch cannons on your maps, switch off the ocean for real space maps with your own night sky - and joining a running round on a big map finally works smoothly.

HEADS-UP FOR MAP CREATORS
- Rock masses are rebuilt without holes (see "Other fixes"). Where a wall between a tunnel and the outside is thinner than one grid cell, it now opens up with a clean edge. If your map has rock masses with thin walls, fly it once in the editor, bake the light again and publish a new revision, so other players get the same shape.
- Maps with more than about 6,000 networked objects (scattered items, luggage, vines, weather zones …) now work, but the editor, the map check and publishing warn you: they cost loading time for every player. Put items into luggage or scatter fewer of them.
- Everyone in a lobby should update. Late joining, network IDs above 6,000 and stuck limbs are fixed on each player's own PC.

PLANETS AND GRAVITY
- Planets with their own gravity, like Super Mario Galaxy. Select any object, rock mass, terrain or group in the editor and press "Make it a planet": a gravity field sticks to it, and players walk all the way around - upside down on the bottom too -, jump, fall and climb mountains on it while the camera turns with them. Choose sphere or box, radius, strength, a soft edge, an offset center, or "Only pull" (players stay upright and just fall onto it).
- The game follows along: items fall toward the planet, ropes and chains hang toward it, and parachutes, gliders, balloons, jetpacks, rivers, lava streams, pets and your own NPCs use its gravity. Oceans and lava seas can be spheres around a planet, and baked light and openness (storm, cold) are measured along the planet's up.
- Scatter around a planet: scatter areas can cover a whole planet, a cap, a ring/band or a polygon - rays come from outside toward the center and blocks stand upright to the surface.
- The camera turns smoothly when you jump from one planet to the next, and the editor camera can follow planet gravity (switch under the view cube), so placing things on the side of a planet is easy.
- New rock mass shape "Sphere" for whole planets.
- Works for every player without extra network traffic; other players no longer get stuck inside a planet on your screen after a fast flight.
- New effect zone "Gravity (jumps)": 50 % lets you jump twice as high and fall slowly, 17 % feels like the moon, 200 % keeps you close to the ground. Jump strength follows gravity automatically (or set it up to 1000 %), and fall damage follows the height you actually fell.

ITEM ABILITIES
- The item creator has a new step "Abilities" - up to four per item, combinable with any effect and the light (a shotgun with a recoil jump, for example):
- Move yourself: cannon, rocket jump, double jump and charge-up launches; jetpack thrust; balloons tied on a string above your head; umbrella and glider; a parachute that opens by itself after 1.5 s of free fall; a rocket pack with a fuse that teammates (or you with G) light on your back.
- Put things into the world: ropes, anti-ropes and rope spools, chains like the chain launcher, a grappling hook that pulls you to a wall or a teammate to you, cloud, shelf or bounce shroom platforms (also thrown), and pitons.
- A placeable cannon like the Scout Cannon, with your own model, force and barrel angle; a yellow arc shows where you will land.
- Wear it: hold an item like any game item, or wear it on the back like the jetpack, backpack or rocket pack, or on the belt like the fanny pack. Custom backpacks and fanny packs get 1-4 slots, their own weight and optionally visible contents.

JUMP PADS AND LAUNCH CANNONS
- Make any solid object a jump pad (Inspector › "Make it a jump pad") or give the game's Scout Cannon your own force, angle and fuse. The invisible effect zone "Launch (jump pad)" throws players for updrafts, ramps and loops.
- Yellow arcs in the editor show every trajectory. Pads and zones can be unlocked by flags, and the camera stays steady during long tumbling flights.

SPACE MAPS AND NIGHT SKIES
- Map › "Ocean & abyss" switches off the game's water and the floor at sea level - nothing but sky above and below. Falling below the abyss height counts like an elimination zone (lives, spectator spot; back to the last safe spot in a test run).
- Sky › "Stars & space": your own stars, aurora with two colors, shooting stars, milky way and nebula, each with strength and color, plus how lively the sky moves.

ZONES, FOG AND SCATTERING
- Spherical effect zones (shape bar "Sphere"): cold, fog, darkness, gravity or music as a shell around a planet; "Snap to planet center" puts it right on one.
- Fog volumes from far away: "Full strength up to" (or "Full strength at any distance") keeps a fog body dense and shaped kilometers away, even beyond the game camera's 1,500 m view distance. Old maps look as before.
- Scatter areas can avoid placed objects ("Avoid placed objects"): no more trees on platforms, roofs or temple tops - also around planets and in surface mode.
- Objects without a collider ("No collider (visual only)") for light pillars, beams, stars and veils.
- Counter display: Map › Game mode › "Counter display" shows a bar at the top ("Star shards ●●○○") and announces every increase to all players - also when a character counts in a conversation.

EDITOR
- Align tool (L), like in Tinkercad: select several objects and line them up at an edge or the center on any axis; click one of them to keep it fixed and align the others to it.
- View menu: "Show gravity fields" and "Show teleporters".

WORKSHOP AND LOBBY
- Players in a lobby can browse maps and suggest them to the host. Guests now see the MAPS tab at the airport terminal and get "Suggest to host", which posts the map to the text chat (and pops up for the host if the workshop is open). Only maps that are public online can be suggested; only the host picks and starts.
- The workshop opens much faster and pages through long lists. The first page shows right away, the rest loads in the background, thumbnails only load for the tiles you see, and "My stuff" and the trash got page buttons. Creators with many private uploads no longer wait for each one to load.
- Browsing the workshop creates about 90 % less memory garbage, so small hitches are much rarer.

MULTIPLAYER FIXES
- Players joining a running round no longer fall into the void. On maps with several stages they were pulled back to the start spawn, whose stage was already hidden for them. They now join the others or the current base camp, and nobody is ever moved to a spot with nothing underneath.
- Joining via lobby, code or invite builds the same world as the host again. The build used to start before the player was in the room, searched every scatter area (minutes instead of seconds) and could end up different, kicked or disconnected. Re-joining also no longer misses the host's character or room items.
- No more 30-160 s freeze after joining a big map. Thousands of queued item registrations in Photon are now applied in one pass, and messages that piled up during the build are processed in small portions.
- Maps with very many networked objects work again: bells, luggage, rain zones and picked-up items failed above about 6,000 networked objects (in multiplayer, items could even turn into other items). Extra network ID ranges now cover up to about 100,000.
- Clicking into the BepInEx console window no longer freezes the game. Windows' QuickEdit made every log line wait until Esc - the game stood still and got disconnected. QuickEdit is now off for that console only (selecting text still works via the window menu › Edit › Mark; config [General] ConsoleQuickEditOff).

OTHER FIXES
- Hands, feet, head and held items no longer get stuck in custom terrain. Ground terrain is now only solid from the outside for bodies and items, like the game's own rock; climbing, footsteps and scattering are unchanged.
- Rock masses no longer show small holes in thin tunnel walls and on rough planets (triangles were turned inside out); walls thinner than one grid cell open up with a clean edge.
- Riding moving platforms is steady - you no longer slowly slide off, also on orbiting moons.
- Teleports, spawns and revives land on the intended floor instead of on a neighboring object or inside a hollow model.
- Maps with rock masses load faster: they replay the saved scatter placement like other maps.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- Planets and item abilities are tested mostly with two players so far; please report anything odd on Discord.
- On planets, custom enemies like spiders still fall toward world down, and grass only grows on top.
- Thin platforms, floors, walls and ceilings can still hold a hand or an item now and then.
