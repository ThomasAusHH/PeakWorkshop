King of the Hill on a 600 m mountain, free-for-all PvP with suitcase loot, story adventures with talking NPCs and boss fights. And when you have seen it all: build your own map right inside the game and share it with one click.

**Normal PEAK stays exactly as it is.** Peak Workshop lives behind its own sign in the main menu.

- **Real lobbies** for up to 16 players: browse open lobbies with map preview, mode and player count, or join a private one with its **join code**.
- **Teams** - the host sorts players by hand or shuffles them randomly. **Friendly fire** on or off.
- **Shared start** - everyone loads the map, then a countdown starts the round for all at once.
- **Late joiners** can watch as a ghost or jump right in - the host decides.
- **Friends get the map automatically** when they join. No hunting for files.
- Host tools: kick and ban, change lobby settings mid-round with **F6**.

- **Zombie waves**, **obstacle courses** with spinning and swinging platforms, **sky islands** - and classic co-op climbing.

<a id="workshop"></a>
<h2><img src="{BASE}/media/stickers/workshop.png" height="43" alt="The in-game Workshop"></h2>

<p align="center"><img src="{BASE}/media/gifs/11_asset_viewer.gif" width="49%" alt="3D viewer turning an imported Yeti"> <img src="{BASE}/media/screenshots/10_workshop_map_page.jpg" width="49%" alt="Workshop page of Crashfall Valley"></p>

Like the Steam Workshop - but inside PEAK.

- **Maps, 3D assets, items and NPC packs** in one place, sorted into categories.
- **Rate, subscribe, download** with one click. Update badges tell you when a creator improved something.
- **3D viewer** - spin and zoom any model before you use it.
- Publish your own creations **public or private**. Private content stays out of every list, but your lobby can still play it.

<a id="build-it-yourself"></a>
<h2><img src="{BASE}/media/stickers/build_create.png" height="43" alt="Build it yourself"></h2>

<p align="center"><img src="{BASE}/media/gifs/08_editor_forest.gif" width="80%" alt="Drawing an area in the editor and a forest pops into it"></p>

- A full **map editor inside the game** - no Unity, no extra tools.
- **Draw an area, fill it with a forest** (or palms, ruins, flowers ...) in one step.
- **Sculpt terrain**, place hundreds of original PEAK blocks or your own 3D models (GLB, glTF, OBJ, FBX).
- **Moving platforms**, traps and hazards, ball throwers, custom skies and music zones.
- Spawn points, goals and loot - then press **P** to test your map on the spot.

<h2><img src="{BASE}/media/stickers/get_started.png" height="43" alt="Get started - installation"></h2>

**Recommended:** install [Peak Workshop on Thunderstore](https://thunderstore.io/c/peak/p/{TEAM}/PeakWorkshop/) with **r2modman** or the **Thunderstore Mod Manager**. BepInEx comes along automatically.

<details>
<summary><b>Manual install</b></summary>

1. Install [BepInExPack for PEAK](https://thunderstore.io/c/peak/p/BepInEx/BepInExPack_PEAK/).
2. Copy `PeakWorkshop.dll` to `PEAK/BepInEx/plugins/PeakWorkshop/`.
3. Start PEAK. The **Peak Workshop** sign drops into the main menu.

</details>

## Requirements & compatibility

- **Everyone in a lobby needs Peak Workshop.** Mod lobbies are separate rooms, so friends without the mod won't see or join them.
- Maps, assets and items your lobby needs download automatically when you join.
- The online Workshop and the lobby browser need an internet connection. Solo play and the editor work with your local content.
- **Not compatible with TerrainRandomiser.** It breaks the map build (maps look half-finished). Disable it while you play Peak Workshop maps.
- Other mods that change map generation may conflict as well. If a map looks broken, try without them first.
- English and German interface (switch in the Workshop panel).

<details>
<summary><b>Online features & privacy</b></summary>

The online Workshop and the lobby browser run on the Peak Workshop server. To use them, the mod sends:

- your **Steam ID and Steam name** - to sign you in, show who uploaded content and who hosts a lobby,
- **the content you publish** (maps, 3D assets, items, NPC packs, thumbnails) and your ratings, subscriptions and reports,
- **lobby details** while you host (name, map, mode, region, player count).

The server keeps your IP address with sign-in sessions and reports to stop spam and abuse. No passwords, no payment data, no tracking.

**Content rules:** only upload what you made yourself or are allowed to share. Every 3D asset needs its origin and license. No hateful, sexual or illegal content. Use the **Report** button on any Workshop page - creators see why their content was reported (never who reported it), and reported content can be hidden or removed.

</details>

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Does it change normal PEAK?</b></summary>

No. Host Game and Play Offline stay 100 % vanilla. The mod only kicks in when you start through the Peak Workshop sign.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do my friends need it?</b></summary>

Yes, everyone in the lobby needs the mod. The maps themselves download automatically.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is it free?</b></summary>

Yes. Peak Workshop and everything in the Workshop are free.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where are my maps stored?</b></summary>

In `PEAK/BepInEx/PeakWorkshop/` (in your mod manager profile folder when you use one). Mod updates don't touch it.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I report a bug or content?</b></summary>

**Content:** open it in the Workshop and press **Report**. **Bugs, feedback and questions:** post them in the {DISCORD}.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why does a map take a moment to load?</b></summary>

Custom maps are built while you load in. Big maps can take a few seconds longer than a vanilla run - grab a snack.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Known problems</b></summary>

- A few screens still show German number formats (like `1,4 s`) in the English interface.
- Some Workshop content still has German descriptions.
- When you publish, the visibility defaults to **Public** - switch to **Private** first if you only want to share with your lobby.
- In the NPC editor, switching an NPC to Fighter, Shooter or Boss clears its dialogue. Set the role first, then write the dialogue.
- Very small 3D models (some packs are 30 cm tall) need the x10 scale step when you upload them.

Found something else? Tell us in the {DISCORD}.
</details>
